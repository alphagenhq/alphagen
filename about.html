About AlphaGen

Independent research, built on official data.

AlphaGen Research is an independent markets research publication covering debt, equities, and macro. We publish for investors who want the signal behind the headline: what a data release, an auction, or an earnings report actually means for markets.

What we publish

  • The AlphaGen Wire: short takes on economic data, Fed and Treasury releases, and company filings, each linked to its source.
  • Movers: AlphaGen's thesis on the day's biggest stock moves, tied to the company's own disclosure.
  • Daily briefs: the Morning Brief before the open, The Close after the bell, and The Week Ahead on Sundays.
  • Chart of the Day: one chart, built from official data, that explains what matters.
  • AlphaGen Notes and the AlphaGen newsletter: deeper research on the themes driving rates, credit, and equities.
  • Data desks: the macro dashboard, Treasury auction tracker, yield curve, and earnings scorecard.

How we work

Every figure we publish comes from an official source: a statistical agency, a central bank, the U.S. Treasury, or a company's own filing. We link to that source so readers can check it. Read our methodology and editorial standards.
